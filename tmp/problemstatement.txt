Problem Statement - LernyX

Traditional e-learning platforms often fail to engage students effectively due to their passive, one-way delivery of content. Learners consume videos and articles without immediate feedback, personalized guidance, or built-in, hands-on practice environments. This decoupling of learning and assessment leads to lower retention rates and slower skill acquisition, especially in technical fields like programming.

Furthermore, when students struggle with specific concepts, standard FAQs or delayed forum responses provide little immediate support, leaving gaps in their understanding. There is a lack of integration between instructional material, practical environments, and personalized mentorship.

LernyX addresses this by providing an all-in-one, AI-powered educational ecosystem. It leverages generative AI (Google Gemini) to instantly convert passive educational videos into interactive quizzes ("Video Intelligence"), while simultaneously offering an in-browser code compiler for immediate practice without setup friction. To supplement this, LernyX includes a pervasive, context-aware conversational AI Tutor using Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) to provide 24/7 personalized debugging and conceptual guidance, ultimately closing the gap between learning, practicing, and mastering material.
