Revolutionizing Technical Education and Interview Preparation

Problem Statement

In the current landscape of online technical education and interview preparation, learners and educators face several critical challenges that reduce learning effectiveness and efficiency.

1. Passive Learning Experience

Most students consume educational content, such as technical videos on platforms like YouTube, in a passive manner. There is often no immediate way to assess understanding or apply concepts, resulting in poor engagement and lower knowledge retention.

2. Fragmented Preparation Process

Technical interview preparation is highly fragmented. Learners are required to use multiple platforms separately for conceptual learning (videos), coding practice (DSA), and aptitude training. This lack of integration creates a disjointed and inefficient learning experience.

3. Assessment Creation Bottlenecks

Educators and recruiters spend significant time manually designing quizzes and coding assessments. The absence of automated or intelligent tools makes it difficult to generate personalized, topic-specific, or adaptive assessments quickly and efficiently.
